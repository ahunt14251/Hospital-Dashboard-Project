# Hospital-Dashboard-Project
Hospital Dashboard Project in monthly and consolidated view

The dashboard was created from the project at the Youtube channel Data Tutorials and the following insights were obtained:
From the start to  the end of the data collected (1/4/2023- 30/10/2024), there was a total of 9,216 unique patients.
The average wait time was 35.30 minutes, the satisfaction score was 4.99 out of 4.99 and the number of patients referred were 3816.

From the referrals obtained, the top referral sources there were 1840 case from General practices, 955 cases from orthopedics, 276 from physiotherapy and 248 from cardiology. 
When looking at the target of seeing patients within 30 minutes of their arrival, it was seen that only 40.68% of patients reached this target. 
In addition to this, when looking at the gender distribution of this, it was seen that there was equitable timely service to both male and female patients, as those patients seen within the 30 minutes of their arrival were 49% females and 51% males.

When looking at admission status, it was seen that there was a divide into those admitted and not admitted which were 50% in both groups, meaning that many were seen and released the same day.

When stratifying patients by race it was seen that most patients are white (2571), followed by African American (1951), multi racial (1557), and Asian (1060). It should be noted that a large number of patients declined to identify their race at (1030). 

Other significant demographic information was patients ages, in which we found most patients (1200) were in the 30-39 years group, followed by young adults between the ages of 20-29 with 1188 patients. the lowest number of patients was in children under 9 years of age with only 1056 cases.

The final summary of these insights reveals there's a large intake of patients including admitted cases as well as referrals, with moderate satisfaction levels and visits from patients from different races and age groups. The busiest times seem to be Saturdays, and Thursdays. With these insights the aim is to optimise resource allocation to improve patient care especially during these busy periods and taking into account the fluctuations in patient admissions and referrals,
